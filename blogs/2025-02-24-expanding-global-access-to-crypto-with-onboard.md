TLDR: Coinbase Wallet has integrated Onboard P2P as an onramp option to make buying crypto easier around the world. Onboard lets anyone purchase crypto with local currency through a peer-to-peer exchange, without lengthy verification, and lower fees. Coinbase Wallet and Base are committed to building a global onchain economy that increases innovation, creativity, and freedom.
