TLDR: Basenames are the fastest way to start building on Base, making it easier to connect, collaborate, and contribute onchain. Get your Basename at base.org/names As more and more people come onchain, wallet addresses aren't cutting it as a way to connect with each other. These long strings of numbers and text add friction to transactions, make it difficult for builders to spot superfans of their apps, and turn finding the right collaborators into a needle-in-a-haystack hunt.
